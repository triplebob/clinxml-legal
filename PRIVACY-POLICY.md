# ClinXML™ Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 16 August 2026 · **Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected and used across the **ClinXML™ family of
tools** — the ClinXML™ Toolkit, the SNOMED Refset Toolkit, and the shared ClinXML™ account and identity
service — and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data
Protection Act 2018.

It is written for our users, who are clinical informatics and healthcare professionals.

## 2. What we never do

- We **do not sell, rent, or share your personal data** with third parties for their own purposes.
- We **do not send marketing email**. Your email address is used only to manage your account and to
  send service and security messages — verification, password reset, and notifications about your
  account or organisation. It is used for nothing else.
- We **do not use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies**, and we do not build profiles of you.
- **We do not look at the content you upload, or the results produced from it.** Those files and
  results are encrypted at rest, scoped to you or your organisation, and are used only to provide the
  features you use. They are not browsed, inspected, mined, or used to develop or train anything.

Access to account information is limited to what is needed to operate, support, and secure the
service — for example to respond to a support request you have raised, or to investigate a security
problem. It is not used for any other purpose.

## 3. The scope of this policy — what we do and do not process

ClinXML™ tools help NHS clinical informatics teams build, analyse, and manage **EMIS clinical search and
report definitions** (and related SNOMED/EMIS code references). The files processed by the tools are
**clinical code and search *definitions*, not patient records.**

- The ClinXML™ tools are **not designed to process patient-identifiable data or special-category health
  data about patients**, and you must not upload patient-identifiable data to them.
- Where you upload content (for example an EMIS XML search definition), **you and/or your organisation
  remain the controller of that content**; we process it only on your behalf to provide the features you
  use, as a data processor.

The personal data we control under this policy is therefore primarily **information about you as a user**
of the service.

## 5. Cookies

We use a single **strictly necessary session/authentication cookie**, set across `*.clinxml.com`, to
keep you signed in and to secure your session.

The tools also store a small amount of information in your browser's local storage — for example
which file you are currently working on, and your display preferences — so that the application
works and remembers your place. This stays in your browser.

Neither is used for advertising or tracking. Because both are strictly necessary to deliver a service
you have requested, they do not require consent.

## 7. International transfers

We choose EEA regions wherever a provider offers the choice. Our application and databases run in
Germany, our file storage and database backups are held in the Netherlands, and transactional email is
sent from an EU region in Ireland. Our hosting provider is contractually committed to processing your
data **exclusively** within the European Union or European Economic Area, and cannot transfer it to any
other country without our prior consent.

Choosing an EEA region is not the whole picture, however. Three of our providers — Railway, Resend
and Cloudflare — are companies established in the United States, and they may access personal data
from there in order to operate and support their services.

Those transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards under our data processing agreement with each
provider: the **UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework** where the provider holds a current
certification under it, and otherwise the **UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard
Contractual Clauses**. Each agreement provides for the Addendum to apply automatically if a
certification lapses, so the safeguard does not fall away.

Transfers to Hetzner in Germany need no such safeguard: the EEA is covered by UK adequacy
regulations.

## 8. How long we keep your data

- **Account data** (your identity, login credentials, and multi-factor settings) is kept for as long as
  your account is active. When your account is closed, or you ask us to delete it, we delete or
  irreversibly anonymise your personal data.
- **Files processed by the ClinXML Toolkit** (the results of a conversion or analysis job) are
  automatically deleted **7 days** after processing. We do not retain your processed output beyond this.
- **Usage records** (a record of actions such as uploads and downloads, used to run the service and to
  produce your organisation's usage reports) are linked to your account only while your account is active.
  When you leave an organisation, or your account is deleted, these records are reduced to **anonymous
  counts** with all personal identifiers removed: while your organisation remains open those counts stay
  attributed to the organisation; once the organisation is closed they are folded into fully anonymous,
  organisation-independent totals. These anonymised totals are no longer personal data and may be retained
  indefinitely.
- **Session and sign-in security records** — the IP address, device/browser, and timestamps associated
  with each session — are kept for the life of that session and removed when it expires or you sign out or
  revoke it. The date of your most recent sign-in is kept as account data for the life of your account.
- **Operational and security logs** generated by our hosting and CDN/security providers (for example
  Cloudflare's edge logs) may include connection data such as IP addresses and are retained for limited
  periods set by those providers.
- **Backups** are cycled out of our systems within **14 days** (daily database backups kept on a 14-copy
  rolling window). Where we delete data, it is removed from our live systems straight away, but a copy
  may remain in a backup until that backup is cycled out — so deletion is complete within 14 days at the
  latest. Backups are not used for any purpose other than restoring the service.

## 9. How we protect your data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- **Encryption in transit** (TLS) for all connections.
- **Passwords stored only as salted Argon2id hashes**, never in plain text. We cannot read your password.
- **Encryption at rest, under keys we hold ourselves.** Files you upload, and the results produced from
  them, are encrypted before they reach our storage provider, and each processing job is encrypted under
  its own key. **Our storage provider holds only encrypted data and cannot read its contents.**
- **Two-factor authentication** available on all accounts, and required for administrative roles.
- **Access controls**, with account information reachable only for the purposes described in section 2.
- **EEA hosting** of the account database and of stored files.

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect personal data
against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

## 10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- request **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request **erasure** of your data ("right to be forgotten");
- request **restriction** of processing;
- **object** to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests;
- **data portability** — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Many of these you can exercise directly in your account (for example, updating your details or deleting
your account). To exercise any right, contact us at legal@clinxml.com. We will respond within one month.
There is normally no charge.
