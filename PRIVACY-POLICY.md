# ClinXML® Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 28 October 2026 · **Version:** 1.2.3

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected and used across the **ClinXML® family of
tools** - the ClinXML® Toolkit, the SNOMED Refset Toolkit, the shared ClinXML® account and identity
service, and our public website and support service - and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data
Protection Act 2018.

It is written for our users, who are clinical informatics and healthcare professionals.

## 2. What We Never Do

- We **do not routinely inspect the content you upload or the results produced from it**. Those files

Access to account information is restricted to authorised personnel and limited to the operational,
support, security, organisational administration, licensing and legal purposes described in this
policy.

## 3. The Scope of This Policy - What We Do and Do Not Process

ClinXML® tools help NHS clinical informatics teams build, analyse, and manage **EMIS clinical search and
report definitions** (and related SNOMED/EMIS code references). The files processed by the tools are
**clinical code and search *definitions*, not patient records.**

- The ClinXML® tools are **not designed to process patient-level records**. You must not upload
  patient-level records, whether identifiable, pseudonymised or otherwise, or special-category
  personal data, unless we have separately agreed in writing to that use and the required processing
  arrangements. Professional names and contact details within permitted work files may still be
  personal data.
- Where permitted uploaded content contains personal data (for example a professional's details in
  an EMIS XML search definition), **you or your organisation determine the purpose of processing**;
  we process it on your or their behalf to provide the features you use, as a data processor.

The personal data we control under this policy is therefore primarily **information about you as a user**
of the service.

## 5. Cookies

We use a **strictly necessary session/authentication cookie**, set across `*.clinxml.com`, to
keep you signed in and to secure your session. Our security and bot-protection services may also use
strictly necessary identifiers to protect the site and its support form.

The website and tools also use browser storage to remember your current workspace or selections and

These technologies are not used for advertising or behavioural tracking. The session and security
identifiers described above are necessary to provide and protect the service.

## 7. International Transfers

We transfer personal data outside the UK to provide the service. Our application and account databases
are hosted by Hetzner in Germany and Finland. Uploaded files and database backups are stored with Railway
in the Netherlands. Those EEA destinations are covered by UK adequacy regulations. Hetzner's agreement commits
it to processing exclusively in the EU/EEA unless we first consent to a third-country transfer.

An EEA storage or email-sending region does not rule out processing elsewhere. Railway's agreement says
its primary processing operations are in the United States. Resend says it stores email data in the
United States; its EU region controls where emails are sent from. Cloudflare processes request traffic
through a global edge network, and its agreement anticipates processing outside the UK and EEA.

Our agreements with Railway, Resend and Cloudflare contain the **UK International Data Transfer
Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses** for transfers requiring that safeguard. Railway's and
Cloudflare's agreements also provide for the **UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework** where
the US recipient has a current certification covering the relevant data. Resend's agreement specifies
the UK Addendum for ex-UK transfers. These providers may use authorised subprocessors under contractual
data protection obligations; contact us for more information about the safeguards relevant to a
particular transfer.

## 8. How Long We Keep Your Data

- **Account data** (your identity, login credentials, and multi-factor settings) is kept for as long as
  your account is active. When your account is closed, or you ask us to delete it, we delete or
  irreversibly anonymise it, subject to the limited licensing and legal-record exceptions below.
- **Terminology licence and acceptance records**, to the extent we hold them, may include your name,
  account contact details, the product and release version used, the terms accepted, when they took
  effect, and the IP address they were accepted from. We retain only the information needed to administer or demonstrate compliance with
  applicable sub-licences, or to establish or defend a legal claim, for as long as the relevant
  obligation or claim period requires. We review the need for these records and delete or anonymise
  them when that need ends, including after account closure.
- **Files processed by the ClinXML Toolkit** (the results of a conversion or analysis job) are
  automatically deleted **7 days** after processing. We do not retain your processed output beyond this.
- **SNOMED Refset Toolkit** exports are generated in your browser and downloaded to your device;
  the toolkit does not store uploaded files or generated exports on our servers. Your device's copy
  is under your control.
- **Usage records** (a record of actions such as uploads and downloads, used to run the service and to
  produce your organisation's usage reports) are linked to your account only while your account is active.
  When you leave an organisation, or your account is deleted, these records are reduced to **anonymous
  counts** with all personal identifiers removed: while your organisation remains open those counts stay
  attributed to the organisation; once the organisation is closed they are folded into fully anonymous,
  organisation-independent totals. These anonymised totals are no longer personal data and may be retained
  indefinitely.
- **Session and sign-in security records** - the IP address, device/browser, and timestamps associated
  with each session - are kept for the life of that session, which is at most **7 days** from sign-in,
  and removed when it expires or you sign out or revoke it. The date of your most recent sign-in is kept
  as account data for the life of your account.
- **Operational and security logs** generated by our hosting and CDN/security providers (for example
  Cloudflare's edge logs) may include connection data such as IP addresses and are retained for limited
  periods set by those providers.
- **Support enquiries and complaints** are kept while we respond and resolve the matter, then for as
  long as reasonably needed to handle follow-up, establish what was agreed, or deal with a legal claim.
- **Transactional email data** held by Resend is retained under its published service retention rules;
  Resend currently states that email and log data are kept for **30 days** on its Free, Pro and Scale
  plans.
- **Backups** are cycled out of our systems within **14 days** (daily database backups kept on a 14-copy
  rolling window). Where we delete data from our live systems, a copy may remain in one of these backups
  until it is cycled out. Backups are not used for any purpose other than restoring the service.

## 9. How We Protect Your Data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- **Encryption in transit** (TLS) for all connections.
- **Passwords stored only as salted Argon2id hashes**, never in plain text. We cannot read your password.
- **Encryption at rest, under keys we hold ourselves.** Files you upload, and the results produced from
  them, are encrypted before they reach our storage provider, and each processing job is encrypted under
  its own key. **Our storage provider holds only encrypted data and cannot read its contents.**
- **Two-factor authentication** available on all accounts, and required for administrative roles.
- **Access controls**, with account information reachable only for the purposes described in section 2.
- **EEA hosting** of the account database and of stored files.

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect personal data
against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

## 10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- request **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request **erasure** of your data ("right to be forgotten");
- request **restriction** of processing;
- **object** to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests;
- **data portability** - receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Many of these you can exercise directly in your account (for example, updating your details or deleting
your account). To exercise any right, contact us at legal@clinxml.com. We will respond within one month.
There is normally no charge.
